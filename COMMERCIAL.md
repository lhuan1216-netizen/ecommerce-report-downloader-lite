# Commercial options

This repository is a functional single-platform Lite edition.

Possible paid services:

- Remote installation and workflow verification.
- Private multi-store and multi-platform edition.
- Scheduled downloads and historical backfill queues.
- Database, spreadsheet, Feishu, or DingTalk integration.
- Maintenance when a supported platform changes its page workflow.

No commercial service guarantees uninterrupted access to a third-party platform. Customers must maintain valid accounts, permissions, and subscriptions and must complete authentication challenges themselves.

Pricing and contact details should be added by the repository owner before publication.
